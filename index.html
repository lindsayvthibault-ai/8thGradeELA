What to Expect from Me

Weekly Email

Parents can expect a weekly email linking them to this website, where the Week at a Glance slide is updated with our current calendar and class activities. This is the best way to stay informed about what's happening in ELA each week.

Conference Period

My conference period is 10:40 AM – 11:35 AM. All phone conferences will be scheduled during this time for parents who need to speak with me directly. I am happy to connect during this window whenever possible.

Email is the Best Way to Reach Me

Email is the most reliable way to reach me during the school day. I check my inbox regularly outside of my conference period and typically respond within 24 hours on school days. You can contact me anytime at Lindsay.Thibault@cfisd.net.

Phone Calls

Consistent communication between home and school is incredibly important to me. While managing a large volume of students means I cannot call every family on a regular basis, please know that if an urgent matter, academic milestone, or specific behavioral situation warrants a conversation, I will absolutely pick up the phone to connect with you. That said, if you have any questions for me, email remains the fastest way to reach me during the school day, and will be the way you hear from me most often.

Partnership

I truly believe that students thrive when we work as a team. You know your child best, and your insights are incredibly valuable to me. Please never hesitate to reach out — whether it's a question, a concern, or a milestone we should celebrate together. I want to partner with you every step of the way this year!

If Your Student Is Absent

Students are required to make up all work missed during an absence and will have the same number of school days they were absent to do so with no penalty. The first step is always to check the Week at a Glance tab on this page to see what was covered and which assignments need to be made up — then follow the process below based on the type of assignment.

✏️

Daily Grades (DGs)

Daily grade assignments can be completed from home. Students should check Schoology for the assignment and submit it within their make-up window. If a student has questions about the instructions, they can request tutoring for additional support.

📄

Major Grades (RAs & Summative Assessments)

Major grades cannot be completed from home and must be made up in Cub Lab (Tue–Thu, 3:05–4:00 PM). If a student needs help understanding the assignment before attending, they can request a tutoring session. See the Cub Lab & Tutoring tab for details on how to sign up.

🙋

Follow Up

Students should follow up with me directly if they have questions about what was missed or what needs to be completed. When late work is submitted, students should send me a message in Schoology so I can grade it and update the grade in HAC promptly. Parents with questions are welcome to reach out via email and I will respond within 24 hours on school days.

📊

How to Check Grades in HAC

Grades are posted in Home Access Center (HAC). Log in at HAC – CFISD using your parent/guardian credentials.

Code What It Means What to Do
Z Zero — work was not submitted. The assignment was due and nothing was turned in. Have your student submit the work as soon as possible. Work may still be accepted if it is not more than 3 school days late. Once submitted, students should send a message in Schoology so I can grade it and update the grade in HAC.
X Exempt / Excused. The student was not required to complete this assignment. No action needed. A grade will not be issued for this assignment — an X averages in as if the assignment does not exist.

Late Work and ‘Redo’ Assignment Indicators

Late assignments may be turned in within three school days of the due date. Teachers will deduct 10 points each day from the grade earned on the late assignment. On the 4th day, the assignment remains a Z. Teachers will make a note viewable to parents in HAC to indicate when the assignment was turned in. This system is a visual tool to help teachers, students, and parents identify when deadlines are not being met.

Decimals are used to track redone assignments. When a student redoes an assignment, the original score is preserved behind the decimal of the new grade, giving parents quick visibility into both scores at a glance.

  • Example: A student scored a 42 on a test and retook it, earning a 68. The gradebook will show 68.42 — the new grade is 68, and the .42 reflects the original score of 42.

Setting Up Grade Alerts in HAC

HAC can send automatic email notifications when your student's grade drops below a threshold you set. Here's how to turn them on:

1

Log in to Home Access Center with your parent/guardian credentials.

2

Click your name or account icon in the top-right corner to open account settings.

3

Select "My Account" from the dropdown menu.

4

Scroll to the "Alert Preferences" or "Notification Settings" section.

5

Check the box for Grade Alerts and enter a threshold (e.g., 70) — you'll be notified whenever a grade drops below that number.

6

Confirm your email address is current, then click Save.

💡Setting a grade alert at 70 is a great way to catch a slipping grade early. You can adjust the threshold anytime in your account settings.
🔁

Redoing Failed Assignments

Students have opportunities to redo or retake assignments on which they earned a failing grade (below 70). The highest possible grade on any redo or retake is a 70. A student may never receive a grade lower than their original grade as a result of redoing an assignment.

⚠️Please note: DPMs (District Performance Measures) and Benchmarks are not included in redo and retest opportunities.

Summative Assessments (Tests)

A student has one opportunity per grading period to retake a test on which they earned a failing grade. Retakes must be completed in after-school Cub Lab or Tutoring.

  • Maximum grade earned on a retake: 70

Relevant Applications (RAs)

A student has one opportunity per grading period to redo a Relevant Application on which they earned a failing grade. Redos must be completed in after-school Cub Lab or Tutoring.

  • Maximum grade earned on a redo: 70

Daily Grades / Class Assignments (DGs)

Teachers will allow students to redo one daily grade or class assignment per grading period on which they received a failing grade. Students should request permission from their teacher to redo the assignment — once permission is granted, the redo may be completed from home.

  • Maximum grade earned on a redo: 70
🐾

Cub Lab vs. Tutoring vs. Targeted Tutorial

Sprague offers three types of academic support outside of class. Here's the difference between them and when to use each:

🐾 Cub Lab

Cub Lab is a supervised setting without direct teacher intervention for students who need to complete, make up/redo, or have extended time on Major Grades (Summative Assessments and Relevant Applications).

Offered: Tuesdays, Wednesdays, & Thursdays, 3:05–4:00 PM

Students must have transportation home arranged by 4:00 PM at the end of Cub Lab.

📚 ELA Tutoring

Tutoring is teacher-led, subject-specific support for students who are struggling to understand a concept or skill. Students can get extra help, ask questions, and receive feedback on their work.

Best for: Students who need additional instruction or practice on a skill.

Tutoring ends at 3:45 PM. Students must have transportation home arranged by this time.

Ms. Thibault's Tutoring Days:

Wednesday – Open Tutorials Thursday – Targeted Tutorials
📝 Permission Required: Students must have written permission to attend Cub Lab or Tutoring, submitted at least 24 hours in advance. Please plan accordingly to ensure your student can participate.

📄 After School Permission Slip

Download, print, and return this form at least 24 hours before your student plans to attend.

⬇ Download Form
🎯

Targeted Tutorial (Thursdays) — Invitation Only

Targeted Tutorial is a teacher-selected, invitation-only session held on Thursdays. Teachers use this time to reteach specific TEKS (Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills) to students who need it.

If your student is invited, you will receive a permission slip sent home and a parent notification by email. This is not a drop-in session — only students who have been identified by their teacher will attend.
